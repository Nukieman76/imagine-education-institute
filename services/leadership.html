Leadership Development

Coaching and academies that strengthen leaders and teams.

Who this is for

School and nonprofit leaders who need training, coaching, and mentorship for the human side of organizational change.

What we deliver

  • 01Leadership academies and coaching designed around local needs.
  • 02Mentorship that is developmental and relational.
  • 03Goal-focused coaching with role clarity, boundaries, and confidentiality.

How this work is done

  • Mentorship is developmental and relational; coaching is typically goal-focused and performance-oriented.
  • Both require boundaries, confidentiality expectations, role clarity, and a process for addressing conflicts or concerns.
  • Leadership training addresses the human side of organizational change.
  • Academies and coaching are designed around local needs and measured by what leaders can sustain.
